# Brined Air Fryer Roast Chicken with Instant Pot Potatoes & Pan Gravy

Serves: 4-6
Prep Time: 20 mins + 2-12 hrs brining
Cook Time: Air fry: 55-65 mins + Pressure cook potatoes: 8 mins

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken (about 1.6-1.9 kg)
- 2 litres cold water
- 120 g salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cracked black pepper
- 1.2 kg potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks
- 1 cup chicken stock or water (for pressure cooker)
- 40 g butter
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1-1.5 cups chicken cooking juices or stock (for gravy)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Steamed greens or peas, to serve (optional)

## Method
1. In a large bowl or container, dissolve the salt in cold water. Submerge the chicken, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight is best).
2. Remove chicken from the brine, rinse quickly under cold water, then pat very dry with paper towel.
3. Rub the chicken with olive oil and season lightly with cracked black pepper.
4. Preheat air fryer to 180°C.
5. Place chicken breast-side down and air fry for 30 minutes.
6. Turn chicken breast-side up and air fry for another 25-35 minutes, or until the thickest part of the thigh reaches 75°C.
7. Rest chicken on a board for 10-15 minutes before carving so juices stay in the meat.
8. While the chicken rests, add potatoes and 1 cup stock/water to the Instant Pot. Pressure cook on High for 8 minutes, then quick release. Drain and lightly season.
9. For gravy, melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in flour and cook for 1-2 minutes to make a roux.
10. Gradually whisk in 1-1.5 cups chicken juices/stock until smooth and thickened.
11. Stir in Worcestershire sauce and soy sauce. Simmer 1-2 minutes, then season to taste.
12. Carve chicken and serve with potatoes, gravy, and optional greens for a family-style meal.

## Timing
4:30 PM - Make brine and submerge chicken | 6:30 PM - Remove chicken from brine, dry and season | 6:40 PM - Start air fryer (180°C) | 7:10 PM - Turn chicken | 7:35 PM - Check doneness and rest chicken | 7:40 PM - Pressure cook potatoes (8 mins) and make gravy | 7:55 PM - Carve and serve
