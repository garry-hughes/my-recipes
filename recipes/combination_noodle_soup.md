Chicken, Prawn & Pork Wonton Combination Noodle Soup

Instant Pot • Kid-friendly base • Adult add-ons optional

Serves: 4-6
Prep Time: 10-15 mins
Cook Time: ~40-45 mins
Total Time: ~45-50 mins

🛒 SHOPPING LIST (WITH QUANTITIES)

🐔 Chicken
- Chicken wings – 300–400 g
- Chicken drumsticks – 4 (≈600–700 g total)

🦐 Seafood
- Raw prawn meat – 300 g (fresh or frozen)

🥟 Freezer
- Easy Tiger Pork & Prawn Wontons – 355 g (1 pack)

🍜 Noodles
- Thick egg noodles – 300 g (Mr Chen's pack is perfect)

🥬 Vegetables
- Bok choy – 2–3 heads (≈300–400 g)
- Brown onion – 1 medium
- Carrot – 1 medium
- Celery – 1 stalk
- Spring onions – 1 bunch

🧄 Aromatics
- Garlic – 4 cloves
- Fresh ginger – 30 g (thumb-sized piece)

🧂 Pantry / Seasoning
- Light soy sauce – 1½ tbsp
- Fish sauce – 1 tsp (optional, subtle)
- Salt – 1 tsp (adjust later)
- Sesame oil – ½–1 tsp (finish)
- White pepper – ¼ tsp (recommended)

🌶️ Optional Adult Table Add-Ons
- Chilli oil / chilli crisp
- Extra fish sauce
- Black vinegar

🍲 COOKING INSTRUCTIONS (INSTANT POT ONLY)

1️⃣ Build the Chicken Broth
Time: ~40 minutes (mostly hands-off)

Add to Instant Pot:
- Chicken wings (300–400 g)
- Chicken drumsticks (4)
- Onion, halved
- Carrot, roughly chopped
- Celery, roughly chopped
- Garlic, smashed
- Ginger, sliced
- Light soy sauce (1½ tbsp)
- Salt (1 tsp)
- Water – 1.8–2 L (just covering everything)

Cook:
- Pressure HIGH – 30 minutes
- Natural release – 10 minutes, then vent

Open lid:
- Remove wings, bones, veg
- Shred meat from drumsticks and return it to the pot
- Taste broth → should be savoury but gentle.

2️⃣ Cook the Wontons (No Pressure)
Time: 5–6 minutes

- Switch to Sauté – LOW
- Bring broth to a gentle simmer (do not boil)
- Add wontons
- Cook until:
  - Floating
  - Wrappers translucent
  (≈5–6 min frozen, 3–4 min fresh)

3️⃣ Bok Choy + Prawns
Time: 3–4 minutes

- Add bok choy → 2 minutes
- Add raw prawn meat → 1–2 minutes, until just opaque
- Turn heat off immediately

4️⃣ Noodles
Time: 3–5 minutes

- Add egg noodles directly to the hot broth
- Let sit until tender (no pressure, no boil)

5️⃣ Final Lift (Do This Last)
- Sesame oil – ½–1 tsp
- White pepper – ¼ tsp
- Optional: fish sauce – ½–1 tsp
- Stir gently.

🍜 SERVING ORDER (BEST TEXTURE)
1. Noodles
2. Wontons + prawns
3. Shredded chicken
4. Broth
5. Spring onion garnish

⏱️ TOTAL TIME
- Prep: 10–15 min
- Cooking: ~40–45 min
- Start to bowls: ~45-50 min

🧠 FINAL NOTES
- Kid-safe, clean flavour base
- Adults customise at the table
- No wasted chicken
- True "combination soup" balance
