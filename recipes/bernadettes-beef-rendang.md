# Bernadette-Style Instant Pot Beef Rendang

Woolies jar paste. Breakfast pressure cook, fridge all day or overnight, evening reduce.
Aimed at Waroeng Bernadette Seminyak: tender beef, dark sauce that clings, not a dry Padang fry and not an oily curry.

The original Ultimate Instant Pot Beef Rendang is left unchanged.

## Why this version exists

- Sear the beef in 2 batches first, take it out, then cook onions and paste. Stops the Instant Pot burning the paste under a pile of meat.
- Deglaze after searing so the Burn warning does not trip.
- Coconut milk, not cream.
- Fridge rest so fat sets and flavour settles.
- Evening skim + toasted coconut + lid-off reduce until the sauce coats.
